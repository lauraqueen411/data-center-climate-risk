Case 1 annual mean power usage effectiveness (PUE) with the supply-air relative humidity upper setpoint at 60% (grey) and 77.5% (black), the lower bound and midpoint of the Lei and Masanet (2022) range. (a) Historical (1985–2014) value. (b) Mid-century change (2045–2074 minus 1985–2014). Ecoregions are ordered from left to right by historical annual mean afternoon wet-bulb temperature: Eastern Cascades Slopes and Foothills (ECSF; n = 1), Blue Mountains (BM; n = 13), Columbia Plateau (CP; n = 61), Klamath Mountains (KM; n = 1) and Willamette Valley (WV; n = 33). Points are the ecoregion mean of facilities averaged over 20 climate models (SSP3-7.0); bars are the interquartile range (25th–75th percentile) of the ecoregion mean across models. All other results use the 77.5% setpoint.
